Section 1

What this covers

v6 is a full pass over the CSP's quality and earnings surfaces. It answers two separate questions on two separate screens, which earlier versions had mixed together.

QuestionScreen
"How is my service doing?"सेवा स्थिति
"Why did I earn this?"कमाई, and the bonus sheet inside it

The bonus is the hinge between them. It is caused by quality but paid through earnings, so it is explained in earnings and only referenced from quality. There is no standalone bonus screen.

Screens in scope

App home · सेवा स्थिति · metric drilldown · नया काम · कमाई · मेरा नेटवर्क · मेरा बिज़नेस · side nav.

All Hindi text in this document is final user-facing copy. English is explanation, not copy.

Section 3

Bonus logic

This is the part most likely to be misread, so it is spelled out fully.

Two independent gates

  1. Eligibility gate — quality. All four quality checks must pass. If any fails, no bonus.
  2. Rate gate — network. Given eligibility, the amount per recharge is set by the combination of the two network categories.
RateCondition
₹120 / रिचार्जदोनों क्वालिटी बहुत अच्छे, या एक अच्छा और एक बहुत अच्छा
₹100 / रिचार्जदोनों क्वालिटी अच्छे हों
₹80 / रिचार्जएक क्वालिटी बेसिक और दूसरा बहुत अच्छा या अच्छा
बोनस नहींदोनों क्वालिटी बेसिक हों

This is why the ledger shows 18 × ₹120 for one cycle and 12 × ₹100 for the next. The rate moved, not the volume.

Three headline states, resolved by priority

Quality failing outranks network dragging. Same order everywhere it is shown.

PriorityStateTrigger
1बोनस के लिए योग्य नहीं हैंany quality check failing
2बोनस खतरे में हैquality fine, network बेसिक
3बोनस मिलने की राह पर हैंboth fine

The same three strings appear in three places, always in agreement: the बोनस row under इसका असर क्या है, the bonus card on कमाई, and the header of the bonus bottom sheet.

Dev note

Compute this state once, server-side or in one shared selector. Do not re-derive it per screen. Three surfaces disagreeing about the bonus is the single most damaging bug this feature can ship.

4.3 · Metric drilldown (bottom sheet)

Opened from any metric row. Title matches the metric name exactly.

Contents: three KPI cards (total / met / missed, each with count and percentage), a one-line cause when the metric is failing, and an advice line. Freshness line at the foot — डेटा 5 अगस्त तक का है।

No subtext block and no trend-line card at the foot. Both were removed as noise.

4.5 · कमाई

  • Total first, big, alone in its card — इस महीने की कुल कमाई ₹9,540. No breakdown inside the total card.
  • Then one card per source, starting with इंस्टॉलेशन — इंस्टॉलेशन · रिचार्ज · बोनस · नेट-बॉक्स पिकअप · कैरी फी.
  • Each expands to show its calculation (10 × ₹300 + 3 × ₹500 = ₹4,500) and a note.
  • The बोनस card shows the cycle (16 - 31 जुलाई) and the bonus state string from Section 3. Tapping it opens the bonus sheet; the ? opens the bonus help sheet.

4.6 · Bonus bottom sheet

The only place bonus status is explained. The header is the state from Section 3 with matching tint and icon, plus a subtext naming where to fix — सेवा स्थिति और नेटवर्क में ज़रूरी सुधार करें, or ऐसे ही काम करते रहें when clean.

Below it, two rows, always both shown. Each has a title, a status subtext, a tick or error mark, and a chevron into the relevant screen.

Bonus help sheet — the ?

Titled बोनस कब और कैसे मिलता है, scrollable.

  • बोनस कब मिलता है — सेवा स्थिति की चारों क्वालिटी सही होने पर आप बोनस पाने के लिए पात्र होते हैं।
  • बोनस कितना मिलता है — the rate depends on both network qualities; shows the four-row rate table with the partner's current row highlighted.
  • Closing note — बोनस हर महीने की 1 और 16 तारीख को आता है।

4.7 · मेरा नेटवर्क

  • चालू कनेक्शन 18/45, with the line इन्हीं कनेक्शन की क्वालिटी से आपका बोनस बनता है. No sector line.
  • नेटवर्क की सेहत — state mark and count, freshness date below the heading, and the line ये दो चीज़ें तय करती हैं कि आपको कितना बोनस मिलेगा.
  • Two parameter rows — ऑप्टिकल पावर and पीक टाइम पर स्पीड — each with its value, its category tag (बहुत अच्छा / अच्छा / बेसिक) and the threshold for the next category. Tapping a tag opens the category table.
  • नेटवर्क की कैटेगरी sits inside an expandable card rather than as a prominent section, and opens the same 3×3 threshold table used on सेवा स्थिति.
  • Each parameter drills into its affected connections — WBOX ID, location, reading, and customer detail.

4.8 · मेरा बिज़नेस and the side nav

New page, reached from a single side-nav entry. Four cards in a 2×2 grid — मेरा नेटवर्क · टीम · स्कीम्स · ज़ोन.

Only मेरा नेटवर्क is wired; the other three render without affordance since those screens do not exist yet. Back from मेरा नेटवर्क returns here, not to home.

Side nav — नेट बॉक्स · मेरा बिज़नेस · सेटिंग्स · मदद (was ट्रस्ट लाइन). Everything except मेरा बिज़नेस is presentational in the prototype.

Section 7

Prototype harness

The published file is self-contained — no build step, no network calls, opens in any browser.

  • Debug panel — bug icon in the top bar. Two rows: SCREEN (App home · मेरा नेटवर्क · कमाई · Quality OS · मेरा बिज़नेस) then HOME · STATE (ध्यान दें · सब बढ़िया हैं · सुधार ज़रूरी है). Changing the state re-renders every screen from the shared model.
  • URL params for direct linking — ?s=<screen>&h=<state>, plus m= for a metric drilldown and help= for a help sheet.

The structural change

Today सेवा स्थिति answers two questions at once — how am I doing, and what is my bonus worth. v6 splits them. Quality states the consequence in one line; the mechanism and the network parameters that drive the payout rate live where the money is, in कमाई and मेरा नेटवर्क.

Cross-cutting

  1. One bonus state, three surfaces. Today the bonus is described differently in Quality (a red banner) and in Earnings (nothing at all). v6 computes one enum and prints it in Quality, on the कमाई card, and in the sheet header.
  2. Quality and money separated. Quality answers "how am I doing"; earnings answers "why did I earn this". Neither screen tries to do both.
  3. Consequences made explicit. Both नया काम and बोनस now state what happens, in the partner's own terms, from the screen that caused it.
  4. Freshness dated everywhere. Every surface reading from the cycle carries its own "…तक" line, in one consistent position.

Section 10

Open decisions

These two are product calls, not design ones. The prototype takes a position on each; both need confirming before build.

1 · Does AT_RISK block the bonus?

The prototype says yes — one failed quality check makes the partner ineligible. This is stricter than what the app implements today.

If the real rule is softer (a reduced rate rather than zero), the AT_RISK bonus row and the sheet header both change.

2 · Does poor quality stop new work, or only slow it?

Quality copy says नया काम रुक सकता है ("may stop"), while capacity_offer__quality.json sets connection_eligible: false outright for NON_COMPLIANT.

One of the two should move — either the copy commits, or the flag softens.

Unbuilt, deliberately

टीम, स्कीम्स and ज़ोन are named on मेरा बिज़नेस but have no screens.

No debug case produces "network failing while quality is fine", so बोनस खतरे में है never appears as a headline in the prototype — it shows only as row 2's status. A fourth case is needed to review that state.
